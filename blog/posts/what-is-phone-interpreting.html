What Is Phone Interpreting?

Phone interpreting is a service where an interpreter joins a call and relays speech between two people who do not share a language. It lets a business serve a caller in almost any language without hiring a bilingual agent for every queue.

How phone interpreting works

The agent places or receives a call as usual, then brings in an interpreter who listens to each speaker and repeats the message in the other language. The pattern is common in customer support, healthcare intake, banking, and call center operations, where the same questions come up across many languages.

Where AI voice interpretation fits

Translatigo runs real-time AI voice interpretation in the browser, alongside whatever web-based dialer the team already uses, so agents do not have to switch systems. Pricing is $0.07 per minute, and new users get a free 20-minute trial to test the setup on real calls before paying anything.

Which dialers it works with

Because it runs in the browser, it works with any web-based dialer or softphone, including platforms such as Avaya, Five9, Genesys, and RingCentral. Setup means installing the Chrome extension and opening the dialer as usual.

Current limitations

Translatigo does not yet have named or certified integrations with specific dialer vendors, and it currently runs in Chrome. Organizations with legal or medical interpreting requirements should keep certified human interpreters in the loop for those calls.
